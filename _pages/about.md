I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the <a href="https://mila.quebec/en/">Quebec AI Institute (Mila)</a> and <a href="https://cs.mcgill.ca/">McGill University</a>, where I am supervised by Prof. <a href="https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~jcheung/">Jackie Chi Kit Cheung</a>. I am also a member of the <a href="http://rl.cs.mcgill.ca/">Reasoning and Learning (RL) Lab</a>.

My research interests broadly span artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on natural language processing (NLP) and reinforcement learning (RL). During my Ph.D., I interned at Google Research (Mountain View), Microsoft Research (Montréal), and Borealis AI (Toronto).
